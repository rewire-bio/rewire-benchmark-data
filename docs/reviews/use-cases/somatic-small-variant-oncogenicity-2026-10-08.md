## Disposition in brief

1. The stored evidence is adequate for the current decision and is unchanged. The one primary source behind it (OncoVI, J Mol Diagn 2026, PMC13269341) was re-fetched; its XML is byte-identical to the stored artifact (SHA256 `1ced323acfac29704bccfd990cc57692770c324ae598983c6973d271aaaf48b2`). All 27 stored result values, numerators and denominators were checked against the text and match (table below).
2. What the evidence measures: agreement of an automated rule implementation (OncoVI) with three different references, on three different populations. It is classification agreement, not driver detection, not a functional effect call and not treatment actionability.
3. Four reference problems limit transfer, all from the paper itself: the 135-variant expert set was chosen after seeing the OncoVI-versus-MTB confusion matrix and the experts used OncoVI's own resource list; two OncoVI criteria (OS2, SBS2) read ClinVar assertions while the 691-variant ClinVar reference is itself derived from submissions under the same standard; the ClinVar set holds 431 oncogenic or likely oncogenic, 258 uncertain and only 2 benign or likely benign variants; and the large MTB set is labelled for protein-function effect, not oncogenicity.
4. Not reported in the retrieved sections of the sources read (the OncoVI full text and main-text sections of two others; abstracts only for the rest): reviewer minutes, calibration, serious-error rates on a random sample, and any prospective or independent model-versus-expert evaluation. OncoVI describes a point score mapped to classes, not a probability. Full texts and supplements not examined are unknown.
5. New source conflicts found in this pass are recorded unresolved (section 6): 93 versus 94 variants in the guideline evaluation set, OM3 and OM4 criterion descriptions paired differently in OncoVI and in the ClinGen SOP v1.0 PDF, "intersection" versus "union" for the bona fide tumour-suppressor list, and resource versions that differ between the paper and the repository.
6. Disposition: docs-only. No scientific record, mapping or definition change is needed now. The new exact facts (class compositions, the two-benign ClinVar denominator, the 33/75/0 expert agreement split) are documented here. Optional numeric extensions are future work and are not pending required intake. The focused planning issue was reviewed and posted by Codex as [rewire-benchmarks #38](https://github.com/rewire-bio/rewire-benchmarks/issues/38), linked to #25, #3 and #344.

## Findings

### 1. What is being labelled (distinctions)

- Oncogenicity (this use case): whether a somatic variant contributes to tumour initiation or progression, assessed with the ClinGen/CGC/VICC point system. Five classes: oncogenic (10 or more points), likely oncogenic (6 to 9), VUS (0 to 5), likely benign (-6 to -1), benign (-7 or less). The SOP has 12 oncogenic and 5 benign criteria; OncoVI implements 11 and 5, omitting OP2 ("malignancy with a single genetic etiology") because it found no resource (OncoVI Methods).
- Germline pathogenicity is a different classification. ClinVar stores germline, somatic clinical impact and somatic oncogenicity as three separate classification types, each with its own terms (ClinVar classification documentation, fetched 2026-10-08T11:18:25Z, SHA256 `0d287538dc6163aec153f1ddb204cf25d3a58317cbf7d05205c43904e2709e5e`). OncoVI's OS2 and SBS2 criteria read ClinVar "clinical significance" values (Pathogenic, Likely pathogenic, Benign, Likely benign, and a listed set of other terms) together with a review status, per its Methods; whether those are the germline or the oncogenicity field is not stated in the text.
- Functional effect: the Erlangen MTB labels record "effect on the wild-type protein function" in five pathogenic-to-benign classes. The paper says MTB variants "had not been assessed for oncogenicity". Agreement with them is a proxy.
- Driver detection (recurrence-based, as in cancer-driver tools) and treatment actionability (AMP/ASCO/CAP tiers, OncoKB or CIViC levels) are separate endpoints. The ACGS guideline states the same split: biological classification first, then a separate actionability assessment that depends on geography and licensing. The npj Precision Oncology LLM benchmark (section 5) is an actionability-level task.
- Origin: tumour-only sequencing does not establish somatic origin. The OncoVI pipeline filters out variants with global gnomAD frequency of 0.025 or higher before classification and keeps only variants with a VEP coding consequence; the paper does not report how many variants of the 93, 7802 or 691 sets this removed.

### 2. OncoVI versions, scope and tuning (primary, section by section)

Versions: medRxiv preprint 10.1101/2024.10.10.24315072 (2024-10-10, CC BY-NC-ND per the Europe PMC record; full text HTTP 403 on ordinary curl at 11:17:34Z, not retried). Journal article 2026 (above). Both abstracts print 93 variants, 80%, 88%, 7802, 557 patients, 79% and 135; the preprint's body was not read, so differences in methods or counts between versions are unknown.

Resource versions named in the journal Methods: COSMIC v99 (Cancer Gene Census file of 1 May 2024, Cancer Mutation Census of 7 May 2024), OncoKB cancer gene list (accessed 29 April 2024), Cancer Genome Interpreter catalogue of validated oncogenic mutations (accessed 1 February 2024), cancerhotspots.org (26 September 2024), UniProt 2024_02, MutSpliceDB (6 February 2024), VEP 111.0, dbNSFP 4.5a, gnomAD exomes v2.1.1 and genomes v3.1.2, MANE transcripts. ClinVar is read through its API; the API retrieval date is not stated (only ClinVar download statistics dated 12 April 2025 are given). The OS2 and SBS2 criteria are therefore not pinned to a ClinVar version in the text. No software commit is given in the paper (the stored configuration record notes this).

Gene and cancer-context scope: the criteria use gene lists (tumour suppressor, oncogene, "dual role") rather than the tumour type of each sample. The SOP set has 50 variants in six oncogenes, 31 in three bona fide tumour suppressors and 12 in genes the SOP authors call context dependent. The MTB set is pan-cancer (largest groups: cancer of unknown primary 74, pancreatic 62, biliary tract 49, colorectal 45 patients) on a 523-gene panel, uplifted from hg19 to hg38. The OS1 criterion treats a variant in the Cancer Genome Interpreter catalogue as established oncogenic "regardless of context". Mechanism strata are reported only as oncogene versus tumour suppressor and missense versus truncating, and only as accuracy or concordance percentages.

Tuning and independence: the paper says criteria were interpreted and resources chosen "with expert consensus" and that the resulting thresholds (for example gnomAD 0.01, phyloP or phastCons 0.5) were implemented as fixed rules. It does not say whether any threshold or resource was adjusted after viewing the SOP, MTB or ClinVar results. No held-out gene, allelic-series or study audit is reported in the sections inspected. Rules and resources can be tuned, and an evaluation can use held-out sets, even when no model is fitted, so the absence of a report is a gap and not a property of the method.

### 3. The three references and what each can support

**Guideline SOP set (direct mapping).** 93 variants taken by the OncoVI authors from the guideline's Supplementary Tables 1 and 3. SOP classes: oncogenic 14, likely oncogenic 29, VUS 38, likely benign 6, benign 6. Accuracy 75/93; O/LO 38/43; B/LB 10/12. The VUS class therefore has 27 agreements out of 38 by subtraction (75 - 38 - 10), a figure the supplementary figure S2 caption confirms ("27 SOP VUSs with classification agreement"). OncoVI "usually assigned higher scores than the SOP" for O/LO and VUS variants. The paper explains the extra points by OncoVI's use of an external catalogue for OS1 and OM3 (triggered "exclusively by OncoVI") and by ClinVar P/LP for OS2 where the SOP authors used a manual literature search. In the 38 correctly classified O/LO variants OS1 and OS2 were the criteria most associated with a correct call (odds ratios 11.8 and 6.29, with wide intervals: OS1 95% CI 1.24 to 591.71, OS2 0.87 to 75.17; the OS2 interval includes 1 although the paper prints P = 0.036). These variants are public guideline examples chosen and curated by the SOP authors, 10 genes, so this is a development-style check, not unseen-gene validation. The guideline's own evaluation: each variant assessed by at least two curators with consensus at monthly meetings (Horak et al. 2022, PMC9081216, author manuscript).

**Erlangen MTB set (proxy mapping).** 7802 unique variants, 489 genes, 557 individuals enrolled February 2022 to February 2024, TruSight Oncology 500. Labels: three expert cancer biologists, effect on protein function. Agreement 6060/7802, VUS 3955/4092, B/LB 961/2261, O/LO versus P/LP 1144/1449. Note on denominators: 4092 + 2261 + 1449 = 7802, so the three denominators partition the cohort along one axis. The paper's text does not say whether that axis is the MTB class or the OncoVI class. This is left unresolved. Errors named by the authors: 2 P/LP variants called B/LB by OncoVI (both triggered SBS2 from ClinVar benign assertions "at the time of submissions (December 2023)"); 303 P/LP variants called VUS. The paper states "the majority (64%; n = 196/303) of the 303 P/LP variants classified as VUS by OncoVI were truncating (n = 138) or splice site (n = 37) variants". 138 + 37 = 175, not 196. The 196 and the two subtype counts are unreconciled in the source and are preserved as printed; 196 is not treated as an established subtype count. The authors say these variants did not occur in bona fide tumour suppressors, so OVS1 was not applicable; 13 B/LB variants called O/LO, mostly through OVS1. SBS2, which reads ClinVar benign assertions, was triggered in 821 of the 961 concordant B/LB variants.

**Expert reassessment set (proxy mapping).** 135 variants. The paper says they "involved 104 different genes (67 OGs, 61 TSGs, and 7 with an unknown role)"; 67 + 61 + 7 = 135, not 104. The unit and overlap of the role counts are not stated and no inference is made. 63 are truncating and 50 missense. Selection was a posteriori from the MTB-versus-OncoVI confusion matrix: all 2 P/LP-to-B/LB and all 13 B/LB-to-O/LO disagreements, plus random draws from the OncoVI VUS and O/LO cells, with more from MTB P/LP called VUS. The same three experts who made the MTB labels reclassified the variants under the oncogenicity guideline, in three random subsets, each given "the list of the resources used by OncoVI for each criterion", while "blinded to OncoVI implementation of the criteria". Results: OncoVI agreed with experts on 108/135 (80%); 33 agreed O/LO and 75 agreed VUS (supplement captions), which leaves no agreed B/LB variant (108 - 33 - 75 = 0 by subtraction). Historical MTB labels agreed with the experts on 46/135 (34%) and OncoVI agreed with the old MTB labels on 30/135 (22%). The 80% is a rate in a disagreement-enriched sample whose composition by design differs from any cohort; it is not an estimate of population accuracy, and the same experts, resources and prior labels are shared. The authors report that OS2 and SBS2 were more often applied by experts alone and attribute this to the experts' ability to resolve conflicting ClinVar interpretations; they say OncoVI "applied the criteria more stringently", and give IDH2 p.Arg172Thr (5 points in OncoVI, 4 more from OS2 by experts despite ClinVar having no assertion criteria). The two P/LP-to-B/LB variants were reassessed by experts as VUS.

**ClinVar set (direct mapping).** NCBI-calculated oncogenicity classifications as of 12 April 2025: 691 variants (stated as 11% of 6113, a denominator the text does not define) in 227 genes. Composition: oncogenic 58, likely oncogenic 373, uncertain 258, likely benign 1, benign 1. The reference holds 431 O/LO, 258 VUS and 2 B/LB. Accuracy 573/691; O/LO sensitivity 362/431 (84%). Reported: "no O/LO variants were misclassified as B/LB by OncoVI or vice versa". Of the 69 O/LO called VUS, 38 (55%) scored 4 or 5; of the 48 ClinVar VUS called O/LO, 32 scored 9 or 10, which the authors attribute most likely to OVS1 for null variants in bona fide tumour suppressors. With 2 B/LB variants, no precise or generalisable B/LB sensitivity can be estimated, and none is printed for this set. The paper treats ClinVar's oncogenicity classification as ground truth.

**Circularity and ascertainment, as far as the sources show.** ClinVar's oncogenicity labels come from submitter assertions under the same ClinGen/CGC/VICC standard that OncoVI implements, and OncoVI's OS2 and SBS2 read ClinVar assertions with review status. The paper does not report an ablation without ClinVar-derived criteria, the share of the 691 variants in which OS2 or SBS2 fired, or whether any of the 691 submitters used OncoVI. OS1 and OM3 read a catalogue (Cancer Genome Interpreter) that is also a curated oncogenic list. Whether the SOP set, MTB set or ClinVar set overlaps the resources' own contents was not audited in the paper. None of this is evidence of leakage; it is the absence of an audit. COSMIC, OncoKB and Cancer Genome Interpreter label versions are given above; ClinVar API version is not.

- Resource versions in the repository differ from the paper: `resources/README.txt` says resources were generated 23 May 2025 from COSMIC census v102, COSMIC Cancer Mutation Census v99 and OncoKB and CGI downloads of that date, while the paper names 2024 versions (COSMIC v99). It was not reviewed for this dossier and no number from it is used. ## Reviewer effort, serious errors, calibration, prospective evaluation

- Reviewer effort: not reported in the retrieved OncoVI sections. The only workload-adjacent facts are that the SOP's own set was double curated with monthly consensus, the three experts reclassified 135 variants, and the Discussion argues that automation helps. No minutes, throughput or review cost are given there.
- Serious errors: the paper reports specific counts (2 P/LP to B/LB and 13 B/LB to O/LO in the MTB set; no O/LO against B/LB crossings in the ClinVar set; no expert O/LO called B/LB by OncoVI in the expert set). Each comes from one cohort or chosen set and one reference, with no common serious-error definition or interval, and none is generalised here. The retrieved sections describe no abstention beyond the VUS class.
- Calibration: the output is an integer point score mapped to five classes by fixed cut-offs. Score correlations (r 0.87 and 0.7) are computed only on variants classified concordantly, so they do not measure calibration. No probability is described, and no calibration evaluation is reported in the retrieved sections. Uncertainty intervals are absent for every printed accuracy and sensitivity, as the stored limitation says; the only intervals are the odds ratios in Table 1.
- Prospective and independent evaluation: none reported in the retrieved OncoVI sections. The MTB cohort is described as enrolled February 2022 to February 2024 and analysed retrospectively; the ClinVar set is an April 2025 download; the expert comparison shares experts and resources. The Discussion calls for application to further large heterogeneous cohorts.
