# Model pages, batch 6, 2026-11-26

Prepared by Claude agents. No human review is claimed. Two research agents fetched sources and wrote the content, one agent verified it, one applied the verifier's findings, and a separate session checked and applied the result.

## What changed

21 Tier 2 model records gain a `profile`, and 21 of them a `run_guide`. The new source records in `batch.jsonl` pin the files the content was written from. No result, evaluation or claim was added or changed, and no existing attribute was edited. ProtSSN and xTrimoPGLM, left out of batch 5, are in this batch.

Five records selected for this batch are not in it: CNN, GCN, CNN-RNN and Transformer are generic baselines with no release of their own, and GPT-4.1 is a closed general-purpose model. They need a different kind of page.

## Sources

Every file the agents fetched was kept, whatever its licence. Files whose licence allows redistribution are in `artifacts/`. All of them, including the others, were uploaded to the private archive bucket under the sha256 the source record pins, and the archive workflow fetches each source again by its address.

Seven models have a paper that is not openly available or could not be fetched, so their pages rest on the abstract, the repository and the model card: xTrimoPGLM, DeepSequence, LigandMPNN, LM-Design, PredFull, Prosit and UniSpec.

## Structured facts

The content files in `specs/` carry a `structured` list: 57 entries, one per released checkpoint. They are not written to the records in this change.

## How statements were checked

The method is that of batch 1 (`docs/reviews/2026-11-26-model-pages-batch-1.md`). Every copied passage was confirmed present in its saved file by `scripts/enrich/check_spec.py`. A verifier agent that had not written the content judged 643 statements against the saved files and raised 16 findings: 1 contradicted by its source, 9 that went partly beyond it and 6 with no support. All 16 were applied (15 reworded or corrected from the saved file, 1 removed) and the checker was run again. No page in this batch was read in full by the applying session before merge.

## Identity questions left open

- CMLM has no release under its own name. The page describes the `protein_mpnn_cmlm` checkpoint of ByProt, the LM-Design implementation, which is what the benchmark that names CMLM ran.
- The xTrimoPGLM Hugging Face addresses redirect to repositories named ProteinGLM. Three causal checkpoints could not be read.
- ProtSSN: the repository states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and the model card states Apache-2.0. Both are recorded.

## Run guides

All have status `source_reviewed_not_executed`. None was run.
