## Checks

`build.py` checks both hashes and asserts that each count and statement quoted in the records appears in the card text: stage sizes, the `mid_dna_mrna_splice` row, the bioreason and genomeqa RL task groups, the licence, the decontamination statement and the GenomeQA limitations.

## Rulings

- **FineDNA is recorded as a dataset, though it is a training corpus.** It is the first training corpus in the store. Its `scope_note` says no result is measured on it and no protocol uses it. It is there so that training overlap can be checked for Carbon-X and for models trained on GENERator or OpenGenome2.
- **No link from the model to the dataset.** No relation in `shared/omics/relations.ts` runs from a model to its training data. Carbon-X's `training_data` attribute names the dataset record.
- **Carbon-X is recorded as a checkpoint.** It is pinned to the model commit, because the store had no Carbon-X record.
- **Training overlap is a point for readers.** The RL blend contains bioreason, vepqa and genomeqa prompts, the same task families as the benchmarks on the Carbon-X card. The card states that all training data was decontaminated against those benchmarks. The statement is recorded and not checked.

## Gaps

- The Carbon-X evaluation table (BioReason Coding, BioReason Non-SNV, GenomeQA, NextBase, FrontierVEP, against frontier language models) is not extracted.
- The store cannot link a model to its training data. A `trained_on` relation would make the overlap queryable.
