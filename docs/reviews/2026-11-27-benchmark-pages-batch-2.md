# Benchmark pages, batch 2, 2026-11-27

Prepared by Claude agents. No human review is claimed. Two research agents fetched sources and wrote the content, two agents verified it, one applied their findings, and a separate session checked and applied the result.

## What changed

25 Tier 1 benchmark records gain a `profile`, and 15 of them a `run_guide`. With batch 1 this covers every Tier 1 benchmark except the five listed below. The new source records in `batch.jsonl` pin the files the content was written from. No result, evaluation, protocol or claim was added or changed, and no existing attribute was edited.

Not in this batch:

- BenchBench-Cloning and its two sub-sets (protocol development, construct design): a safety classifier stopped the research agent while it was writing the first of them, and none of the three was taken further. The repository the record names returns not found; only the arXiv paper is available.
- HECTOR 2026: the paper exists only as a medRxiv preprint, which was rate limiting, and no repository or published version was found.
- "Where single-cell foundation models help": bioRxiv only, and no repository was found.

## How statements were checked

Every copied passage was confirmed present in its saved file by `scripts/enrich/check_spec.py`. Two verifier agents that had not written the content judged 820 statements and raised 37 findings (4.5%): 7 contradicted by a source, 29 that went partly beyond it and 1 with no support. All were applied and the checker was run again.

The brief gained a rule after batch 1 that a number belongs to the exact set it is quoted for. The rate did not fall: batch 1 was 4.7%. One research agent's 12 pages had 11 findings and none contradicted by a source; the other's 13 pages had 26 findings, 7 of them contradicted. Benchmark pages should not be published without the verifier pass.

No page in this batch was read in full by the applying session before merge.

## Found in the sources themselves

Each is stated on the page concerned.

- SEQC2 HCC1395: the paper prints 346 gain calls; the deposited file at the stated address has 307 rows. Loss and LOH counts agree.
- Brandes et al. 2023 stop-gain benchmark: the counts differ between a figure legend (32,362 and 36,034) and the Methods (32,441 and 36,113).
- OncoVI: the abstract gives 80% and 79% where the Results give 81% and 78%.
- Talos: the abstract's 90% is one trio cohort's figure, and the comparison with Exomiser uses a different number of families.
- MetageNN: the released settings file points at a much smaller training set than the paper describes.
- RNAGym v0.1.1: the script at the pinned commit computes a different metric from the published leaderboard, which now points at another branch.
- Gene-MTEB: the README says four clustering tasks where its own list and the paper give eight; the classification split is one unstratified split in which only the rarest label gets about eight training examples.
- Plant Genomic Benchmark: the dataset card's summary says 32 datasets and its table has 31 rows.
- MPRabc: the model is trained and scored on the same cell line's CRISPRi data, and the paper does not say whether the scored pairs overlap the training pairs.
- Rewire's Evo 2 cloud study: the platform labels in the protocol and in the run differ.
