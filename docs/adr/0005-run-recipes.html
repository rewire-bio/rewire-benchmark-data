Every model and benchmark should carry a how-to that a scientist can run, evidenced the way a result is evidenced, and promotable later into an independent Rewire run. The store already holds the mechanism and almost none of the coverage. This record weighs three ways to close that, picks one, and states the coverage ceiling honestly rather than promising a how-to on every record.

Problem

run_recipes covers 20 of 8,128 records and is barred from models by a validator gate. Of 85 instructions, 84 say they have never been executed, including three that passed a smoke check two months ago.

Decision

Keep one how-to attribute, widen the gate to model and pipeline, make purpose the discriminator, structure four requirement blocks, require a dated receipt bound to the instruction by hash, and record an absence as a reason.

Ceiling, stated up front

First pass 9 model checkpoints and 23 benchmarks, 32 new recipes. Ceiling with today's upstreams about 78 model records of 386 and 38 benchmarks of 128. Everything else carries a measured reason.

Context

Measured in this worktree, over data/entities and the validator source:

  • run_recipes is on 20 of 8,128 entity records: 15 of 128 benchmarks and 5 of 1,562 protocols. That is 30 recipes and 85 instructions. 84 instructions are source_reviewed_not_executed and 1 is executed.

The attribute registry then declares the three run_* keys on benchmark (attribute-registry.ts:34-36) and protocol (:498-500) only. So 0 of 386 models, 0 of 618 methods, 0 of 133 pipelines and 0 of 5,564 configurations can carry a how-to at all.
  • protocol_id and a two-value purpose enum are mandatory on a recipe. Neither has a meaning on a model.
  • requirements is five free-text strings. 12 of the 30 recipes carry the same hardcoded sentence, Not stated in the cited section. Several of these steps expect a GPU., emitted from a single unknownHardware constant at scripts/omics/recipes/projects.ts:67.
  • There is no cost, runtime or verified_on field anywhere, and resource_estimate is hardcoded null at four sites in lib/baseline-coverage.ts.
  • Three smoke checks dated 2026-09-20 sit unattached in data/omics/reviewed/run-recipes/review.json. Two further measurements reframe the problem, and neither is in the brief that started this work.

The model denominator is not 386

The repository id, the pinned revision and the check date are all in one prose string today.

Comparability by equality can never pass

All 24,726 evaluations carry a comparison block. 18 of them are rewire_run, sitting on 7 protocols, and every one of those 7 protocols holds only rewire_run evaluations. Zero protocols carry both a Rewire run and a published number, so protocol_id differs on every real claim and always will, because the harness departs from the published protocol by construction.

The fields are also filled at different rates and hold different kinds of thing. Among author_reported evaluations the comparison block fills protocol_id 13,610 times, budget 2,254 and subset 81. On the 18 rewire_run evaluations the fill is counted here: protocol_id, dataset_version, split and population on all 18, and adaptation, aggregation, budget, inputs, metric_implementation and subset on 14 each. metric_implementation is a 64-hex SDK code hash on our side (08cbbaac…, fcc5ab65…) against a metric name on the authors'. dataset_version is a record ID on our side and a provenance sentence on theirs: Official FLIP2 manuscript; retrieved 2026-09-19; exact bytes pinned. A gate requiring the nine contract fields to match would bind nothing, forever.

Options

AMinimal extension

Admit model to the existing gate and add optional blocks beside the five requirement strings. Every new field optional, a grandfather clause so the 30 existing recipes validate unchanged, and the 791-line hardcoded projects.ts replaced by a declarative manifest.

Pros

  • Cheapest credible path and the strictest reversibility discipline. No record is rewritten.
  • Finds that the three smoke receipts attach with no test relaxed, and that the review.json pin is byte-correct.
  • A declarative manifest is the right shape for curation at scale.

Cons

  • Its coverage arithmetic does not survive measurement: 38 and 187 both count things that are not Hugging Face revisions, and its pilot criterion of four distinct projects cannot be constructed from 9 records that are all bacformer.
  • Silent on the export.ts gap that its own queryability step depends on.
  • Its literature-first check, that a new rewire_run must have no author-reported sibling on the same protocol, binds nothing: none of the 7 protocols has one and none will.

BExecution first

A separate run_spec on the protocol mirroring the harness job manifest field for field, plus model_runtime on the model, a spec_sha256 over a canonically emitted manifest, and a five-disposition comparability block with departs_from.

Pros

  • The highest factual precision of the three. Every measurement checked against the repository reproduced, including validate_job line for line.
  • Its two mechanisms are the only ones that make "promotable into an independent run" literally true: spec_sha256 binds a receipt to exactly what ran and is recomputable with one command and no model, and the disposition block is the only comparability proposal the data supports.
  • Its GPU escalation gate is the first workable encoding of literature-first in either repository.

Cons

  • run_spec applies to 8 harness protocols and to no model, so for 120 benchmarks and every model it falls back on the quoted-README path it declines to invest in.
  • It adds two attributes to a store that already carries run_recipes, run_guide and run_documentation, giving five how-to shapes.
  • It leaves verified_on optional behind a dated cutover, which is the wrong call: a receipt date is not a scientific value and the one existing date is evidenced twice over.

CReader and standardsSpine of the decision

The how-to is a generated reader view: every sentence on the card is a stored value, a skos:definition or a reviewed threshold, with a published coverage counter, staleness measured against release_date, and a nightly pin-drift check.

Pros

  • The only option that measured the surface a scientist reads, and the measurement is a real defect: the website component hand-writes how-to prose, including a hardcoded protocol_id === "mfass-v2-frozen-encoder" branch and a Python adapter snippet that are in no cited source.
  • The only one right about export.ts, and right to keep a leaf-path coverage test given that 5 of the review block's 12 keys already vanish before RDF.
  • Staleness against release_date rather than the wall clock is the only answer compatible with byte-reproducible releases. Its nightly tier is the one mechanism that would have caught the recorded gpn-star case, where the same URL served different bytes seven weeks apart.
  • Its coverage framing, every record carries a recipe or a reason and the ratio is published, is the right published artifact.

Cons

  • The costliest: roughly 2,000 lines over 8 to 10 weeks, two waiver files, three JSON-LD export families and a notebook emitter.
  • Two export targets have no consumer: schema:HowTo since Google retired HowTo rich results, and Croissant, which carries no runnable code.
  • It over-engineers around the website coupling, policing an optional verified_on with a declining waiver file when the fix is five lines in the sibling.
  • It widens the gate to kind: method, 618 records that are algorithms with no artifact to obtain, for no measured yield.

Decision

Take option C as the spine, graft option B's two promotion mechanisms whole, hold to option A's schema discipline and first-pull-request scope, and replace both coverage stories with the measured denominator.

2. The gate

export const runnableSubjectKinds = [...benchmarkSubjectKinds, "model", "pipeline"] as const;
export const isRunnableSubject = (kind: string): boolean =>
  (runnableSubjectKinds as readonly string[]).includes(kind);

run-recipe.ts:118 switches to isRunnableSubject. The registry declares run_recipes: "run-recipes" on model and pipeline, and changes benchmark:36 and protocol:500 from "json" to "run-recipes".

Excluded, with counts. kind: method, 618 records: an algorithm with no artifact to obtain. kind: configuration, 5,564 records: a parameter set that inherits its how-to through configuration_of. kind: service, 6 records: the 35 real hosted services are kind: model at entity_level: service and are already admitted, so no api runtime value is added either; a provider how-to is a curl or python snippet the existing runtime enum covers. kind: pipeline, 133 records, is admitted because a pipeline is a runnable composition, and its yield is measured before curation is spent on it.

That is 308 of 386 model records. unextracted stays for a record whose code location was never read, and unavailable for a record whose source states there is no public code. 4. Four structured blocks, and nothing else

Additive. Every new field optional, and the five requirement strings keep their bytes. Only four things gain structure, because only these four are checkable without a reader.

One rule carries the no-invention discipline: each of the four blocks is either present or named exactly once in requirements.missing, with a reason from the missingness vocabulary. It fires unconditionally for obtain_and_run and harness_run recipes and for every manifest-built recipe, and conditionally for the 30 pre-existing ones, which carry none of the four blocks and so validate untouched. 6. Evidencing an instruction

Unchanged, because it already works: per-instruction source_ids plus source_locator, every cited source a source record on the owner with a 64-hex artifact_sha256. A Hugging Face model card is README.md, so the locator form the existing test enforces fits with no regex change: README.md at 6d2af6ac, How to use, lines 41-58. The deep link for a GitHub source is a blob URL with a #L<a>-L<b> anchor; whether Hugging Face blob pages honour the same anchor is unmeasured, so the Hugging Face link is emitted without one until someone checks.

The grammar moves into one module, shared/omics/run-locator.ts, with one parser and one deepLink(), and the regex now duplicated in tests/omics-recipe-sources.test.ts is imported from it. A link is built, never stored, so a stored record cannot carry a wrong one. The archived bytes are reached the way ADR 0003 reaches them, through the source's primary_capture_id into the capture ledger and from there into ADR 0004's object store, not through a second pointer: source.attributes.artifact is declared and used by 0 of 1,483 sources, and recipes do not make it the join.

7. Comparability, and the departure that is real

A recipe may carry a comparison block over the nine contract fields plus subset, each evidenced. An evaluation's reproduction gains a comparison_basis naming the author-reported evaluation compared against, a disposition for every one of the ten fields, and at least one stated limitation. Enforcement is offline and model-free.

DispositionWhat the validator requires
equalThe two strings match, both ways.
equivalentA note, and the two values must differ.
differsA note, and the two values must differ.
form_mismatchA note, and the two values must differ. This is the honest answer for a code hash against a metric name.
unstatedThe published value is null, empty, or one of unreported, not stated, not reported.
OmittedAn error. All ten fields carry a disposition or the claim fails.

The verdict is derived, never stored: comparable only if the four identity fields (protocol_id, dataset_version, split, subset) are all equal and no field is differs or form_mismatch, except that budget at differs is permitted, because compute budget does not change what is measured.

departs_from is required on a harness_run recipe whose owner is a rewire-* protocol: the published protocol it departs from, which fields, why, whether the departure is closable, and what closing it would take. All 7 protocols carrying a rewire_run are in that position today, and the departure currently lives in a prose description.

Literature-first is encoded as a gate on compute, not as a sibling check. gpu_count > 0 requires an escalation block naming at least one pinned source, a gap statement, and which comparison fields no published evaluation of that protocol satisfies. No named gap, no GPU run, refused by a validator.

8. Export and the reader surface

run_recipes changes registry type from json to run-recipes, a node type, which needs the top-level node-list change in scripts/kg/export.ts described above: one node minted per recipe at <record IRI>/run_recipes/<i>, reusing the nested path that already handles derivation inputs. A leaf-path coverage test asserts that for every recipe in the store the set of JSON leaf paths equals the set the node spec exports, because the review block already proved the silent-drop mode: 5 of its 12 keys never reach RDF.

Four new SKOS schemes in data/vocab (runtime, execution-status, receipt-scope, recipe-purpose) are required anyway, because 17 rbs:*Concept shapes test skos:inScheme. Their skos:definition strings become the sentences the reader sees. That fixes a verified defect: the website component today hand-writes Source reviewed; these instructions have not been executed by rewire., a hardcoded protocol_id === "mfass-v2-frozen-encoder" branch and a Python adapter snippet, none of which is in a cited source.

The card is computed here, in shared/omics/run-card.ts, as a pure projection added to generatorFiles in scripts/serving/sqlite.ts and stored as a gzipped blob in a new run_cards table. No sentence on it is written by a renderer: each is a stored value, a skos:definition, or a fixed sentence from data/ontology/recipe-freshness.json. Staleness is computed against release_date, never the wall clock. The default badge, on 81 of 85 steps after the first batch, says plainly that nobody has checked the commands still work, and the catalogue publishes the ratio as a number.

Exports are cut to what has a consumer. Kept: FAIR4ML as an export view only (fair4ml:usageInstructions, fair4ml:codeSampleSnippet, schema:*Requirements), because it is the only vocabulary with first-class runnable-code properties on a model, and rejected as the stored shape because 0.1.0 has no evidence pinning, no verification date and no release since 2024-10-28. Kept: the Containerfile emitter, which exits 2 naming the missing field rather than guessing a tag.

Consequences

Good

  • Models can carry a how-to, and the absence of one becomes countable for the first time, against an honest denominator: 29 checkpoints, 35 services, 14 methods, and 308 families that are inapplicable by construction.
  • The store stops under-claiming. Three smoke checks that passed on 2026-09-20 attach in the first pull request, taking executed-or-smoke-tested from 1 of 85 to 4 of 85 on evidence that has been in the repository for two months, with every hash re-verified.
  • "Our runs are not comparable to the published numbers" becomes ten typed, queryable facts per claim instead of a sentence in a description. The first measurement of it is itself worth publishing: zero of the 7 protocols carrying a Rewire run has an author-reported sibling.
  • resource_estimate can stop being hardcoded null, so the local_run readiness capability passes on measured evidence rather than a narrative sentence.
  • Recipes become queryable triples and exportable to FAIR4ML, and a recipe locator becomes a deep link built rather than stored, so a stored record cannot carry a wrong link.
  • A recipe's evidence is re-fetched nightly and compared, the one tier that would have caught the recorded gpn-star case where the same URL served different bytes seven weeks apart.

Bad, without softening

  • Coverage tops out near 20 percent of model records, 78 of 386, and only because 308 are families that correctly get a reason. Benchmarks top out near 30 percent. Read literally, "a how-to section for each model and benchmark" is not delivered, and no design delivers it without inventing values.
  • The first model batch is 9 records across three projects, 7 of them bacformer, and 12 checkpoints in all are writable from bytes already in the repository, 8 with both an obtainable weights pin and a readable how-to. The model programme is bacformer-dominated until more pins resolve, but it is not a single-project programme.
  • Two places hold the same hardware fact, the prose string and hardware_floor, and nothing deterministic can check they agree.
  • Every real run above a synthetic smoke needs a pull request in rewire-benchmarks first. If those are slow, the receipt machinery stays decorative, which is the state it has been in since 2026-09-20.
  • Model-owned recipes do not render until rewire-database ships a model how-to surface; its docs state that attributes.run_recipes belongs to evaluation-design records.
  • The nightly resolve will generate a drift backlog nobody has time to close, and the backlog number gets published. A drifted pin never invalidates a recipe, because the bytes are archived, but the number will look bad.
  • CPU-only verification can never say a GPU recipe runs. Every receipt it produces is synthetic_smoke, and a reader who wants "last verified on the hardware the paper used" still does not get it.
  • Changing run_recipes from json to a node type changes the RDF output, so it needs a new release id, and a published bundle asset can never be replaced.

Ongoing cost, for two people

  • Triage the nightly drift list: roughly 1 to 2 hours a week at about 60 pinned recipe artifacts.
  • One review per rewirebench release, because a release changes sdk_code_sha256, which is comparison.metric_implementation, which appends a new version to each harness_run recipe.
  • Keep the CPU smoke green: 2 to 4 hours a month, accepting that a red smoke job never blocks a release.
  • Write a missingness reason for each record a batch touches, forever, rather than one sweep.

Open questions for the owner

This record is proposed, not accepted. Four questions are open, and each is a choice between two stated alternatives.

1. Attribute topology

One run_recipes with purpose as the discriminator, or a separate run_spec attribute on the protocol? One attribute keeps the store at three how-to shapes and folds the harness job into a recipe. A separate attribute gets a strict block of its own and a cleaner validator, at the cost of a fourth shape, and a fifth once model_runtime follows.

Recommendation one attribute.

2. Gate width

model only, or model plus pipeline (133 records, yield measured 2026-11-17 at 31, 23 percent, carrying a 40-hex-pinned code location, 24 of them in their own attributes; 0 carry a weights pin, 0 a container image, and 101 have no code location at all)? kind: method (618) and kind: configuration (5,564) are excluded either way, and the service kind (6 records) needs no change because the 35 real hosted services are already kind model at entity_level: service.

Recommendation model plus pipeline, and the deferral is withdrawn now that the yield is measured. 23 percent is the same order as the 38-of-128 benchmark yield this record already commits curation to, the gate change is one array element, and the model gate reaches none of it: the 7 pipelines whose pinned repository belongs to an upstream model point at 3 model records, all entity_level: family, which this record rules inapplicable. Sequencing goes the other way, though: 12 checkpoints are writable from bytes already in the repository, while each pipeline recipe needs a fetch, a source_ids edit and, for 25 of the 31, a review pass first. Figures: maintenance/reports/adr-open-questions-2026-11-17.md.

3. GPU budget

None, or some, even 200 USD a month? With none, synthetic_smoke is the permanent default receipt scope and no recipe can ever carry a "last verified on the hardware the paper used" claim. With a budget, selected_assay on real weights becomes the default, the escalation gate stays but its approval becomes routine rather than exceptional, and the cost block starts carrying real figures against dated price sources.

Recommendation none stated; this is a spending decision, not a technical one.

4. Sequencing

Given that the first model batch is 9 pinned checkpoints across three projects: start the curation programme with the 8 that carry both an obtainable weights pin and a readable how-to, or start with the 23 benchmarks that have a reachable GitHub repository? Models prove the new gate end to end. Benchmarks give more breadth per hour but prove nothing new about the schema.

Recommendation none stated; both are defensible and the choice is about what the catalogue should show first.
