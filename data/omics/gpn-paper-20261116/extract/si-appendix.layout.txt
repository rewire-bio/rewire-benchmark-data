Table S2: Test perplexity. Perplexity, defined as the exponentiation of the cross-entropy loss, is
equivalent to 1 over the probability given to the correct nucleotide. Arabidopsis thaliana chromo-
somes 4 and 5 were used for validation and testing, respectively. Note that reducing the repeat
weight leads to improved test perplexity in non-repetitive regions, which are often of greater inter-
est. Compared to full down-weighting, moderate down-weighting results in a similar improvement
in perplexity for non-repetitive regions without sacrificing genome-wide perplexity as much.

                   Model                Chromosome-wide          Non-repeat regions
                   Repeat weight 1              2.88                       2.99
                   Repeat weight 0.1            2.90                       2.92
                   Repeat weight 0              3.03                       2.92
